# Campus Complaint Management System

> Project plan for "Campus Complaint Management System". This project was created to solve: "In educational institutions, students and staff encounter various campus-related issues such as damaged classroom equipment, electrical problems, water leakage, sanitation issues, laboratory equipment faults, hostel maintenance problems, and other infrastructure-related concerns. Currently, these complaints are often reported through verbal communication, paper-based forms, or informal messaging. As a result, complaints may be missed, delayed, or difficult to track. Students may also have no clear way to know whether their complaint has been received, assigned, or resolved.

The lack of a centralized complaint management system makes it difficult for administrators to prioritize complaints, assign them to the appropriate maintenance staff, monitor their progress, and maintain a proper record of completed and pending complaints. This can lead to delayed resolutions and reduced transparency in campus maintenance.".
